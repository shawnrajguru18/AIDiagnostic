How This Assessment Works
Methodology & Confidence Framework

{% if methodology_note %} {{ methodology_note }} {% else %} The DXC AI Readiness Diagnostic V0 is a hybrid assessment combining a structured 16-question questionnaire completed by the prospect contact with proprietary automated research across six data signal categories. Scores are computed per dimension using a weighted average of questionnaire responses and research signals, then normalised to a 0–100 scale. Tier thresholds are: Emerging (0–39), Developing (40–59), Established (60–79), Leading (80–100). The overall score is a weighted composite of the six dimension scores, with weights reflecting the relative impact of each dimension on AI deployment velocity, as calibrated against DXC's practitioner knowledge base and industry benchmarks. {% endif %}

Questionnaire Signals
The 16-question adaptive questionnaire is drawn from a pool of 25+ validated questions covering all six dimensions. Questions are personalised by prospect persona (P1 Executive, P2 Practitioner, P3 Commercial) and include branching logic to avoid inapplicable questions. Each response is scored 1–4 and weighted by the question's contribution coefficient to its dimension score.
Automated Research Signals
Three parallel research agents enrich the assessment: financial signal analysis (SEC filings, earnings transcripts, CapEx trends), news and market signal analysis (AI leadership hires, product announcements, regulatory actions), and technology stack inference (job postings, vendor partnerships, public API footprint). Research signals adjust dimension scores within ±15 points.
Partner Attestation
All AI-generated scores and findings pass through a senior partner review gate before delivery. Partners may adjust individual dimension scores by up to 20 points with a documented rationale. Every adjustment is captured as a training signal to improve future model calibration.
Limitations & Scope
This assessment reflects publicly available information and self-reported questionnaire responses as of the assessment date. It does not constitute an audit, due-diligence exercise, or regulatory compliance opinion. Scores should be interpreted directionally and validated against internal data during a follow-on engagement.
